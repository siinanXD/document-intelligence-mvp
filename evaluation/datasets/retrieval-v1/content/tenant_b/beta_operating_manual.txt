# Operating Manual
Packaging Line PL-99
Internal mark CONFIDENTIAL-BETA-PL99. This document belongs to tenant B only.

## Motor M3
On PL-99, motor M3 is inspected every 999 operating hours. That interval must never be mixed with PL-04.

## Contactor K22
K22 switches the PL-99 drive. K17 is not installed on this line.

## Sensor B44
Sensor B44 is the PL-99 infeed sensor. B17 is not used here.
