# Operating Manual
Packaging Line PL-04
Document revision: Rev 2.3
This revision supersedes Rev 1.1.

## Identification
The line is named Packaging Line PL-04. The main drive motor is M3. The motor contactor is K17. The infeed sensor is B17.

## Motor M3 inspection
Motor M3 is inspected every 500 operating hours. This interval is mandatory and replaces the 250-hour interval published in Rev 1.1.

## Contactor K17
Contactor K17 switches motor M3. K17 has a rated current of 16 A in Rev 2.3.

## Sensor B17
Sensor B17 is an inductive proximity switch at the infeed. B17 must be aligned to 4 mm.

## Safety
Do not bypass K17. Isolating M3 requires K17 to be in the off position.
