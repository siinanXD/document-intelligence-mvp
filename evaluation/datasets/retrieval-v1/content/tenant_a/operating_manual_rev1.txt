# Operating Manual
Packaging Line PL-04
Document revision: Rev 1.1
This revision is superseded by Rev 2.3 and must not be used for current maintenance planning.

## Identification
The line is named Packaging Line PL-04. The main drive motor is M3.

## Motor M3 inspection
Motor M3 is inspected every 250 operating hours according to Rev 1.1.

## Contactor K17
Contactor K17 switches motor M3. K17 has a rated current of 12 A in Rev 1.1.
